# 0.9.4 — 2026-07-30

## Added

- `nhtsa_search_complaints` output gains `unreliableIncidentDate: { reported, reason }` (`reason` is `postdates_filing` or `predates_model_year`), populated in place of `dateOfIncident` when a complaint's own filing date or model year rules out the incident date NHTSA reported. Rendered in `content[]` as `Unreliable (<reason>) — NHTSA reported <date>, which <contradiction>` alongside the machine-readable reason. ([#33](https://github.com/cyanheads/nhtsa-vehicle-safety-mcp-server/issues/33))

## Changed

- `getComplaintsByVehicle`'s `resolveIncidentDate` (formerly `normalizeComplaintIncidentDate`) now checks a parsed `dateOfIncident` against the complaint's own `dateComplaintFiled` and queried `modelYear` before accepting it — an incident cannot postdate its filing or predate `modelYear - 1`. Placeholder handling (`12/31/1969`, `01/01/1970`, any 1901 date) is unchanged and still runs first; unparseable values pass through untouched; the `9999` no-model-year placeholder is exempt from the model-year bound. ([#33](https://github.com/cyanheads/nhtsa-vehicle-safety-mcp-server/issues/33))
- `nhtsa_lookup_vehicles` format() now distinguishes "nothing matched" (spelling hint) from "the page overshot a nonempty result set" (`outOfBoundsMessage()`), instead of showing the spelling hint for both. A capped `manufacturer` walk that also overshoots its page appends a note that the count is a floor, not a total. ([#34](https://github.com/cyanheads/nhtsa-vehicle-safety-mcp-server/issues/34))
- `nhtsa_search_complaints` and `nhtsa_search_investigations` `noMatchGuidance` output descriptions now note they also cover an out-of-range page, matching current behavior.
- `docs/design.md` documents a corpus check of the ODI complaint file (`FLAT_CMPL.zip`, 2,230,037 rows, 2026-07-30): 110,144 blank `dateOfIncident` rows, 5,659 across the 1901 placeholder, 684 at `12/31/1969`; 169 rows carry an implausible year, of which the filing-date and model-year bounds together catch 159, and the remaining 10 carry the `9999` placeholder and are unreachable through `complaintsByVehicle`. The earliest incident date the corpus corroborates is 1965-12-01.

## Fixed

- A complaint whose reported incident date contradicted its own record previously either rendered as the real incident date or was silently dropped as if NHTSA held no date at all; it now reports the contradiction instead of asserting or erasing it.
